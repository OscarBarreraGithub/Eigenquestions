This plan defines the next calibration and a path to the entire atlas. Twenty-four requests are now prepared and verified; no model has been launched

## Three different deliverables

1. **Readable region coverage:** review every nonreserved description in every
   selected leaf, with 8–12 actual central/boundary/contradictory source papers per
   manageable family. Preserve exact unit membership and basis per assessment.
   A title plus three central examples cannot certify omitted members. Broad
   parents synthesize distinct child scopes and expose overlap; they need not
   assert one shared mechanism. Every source gets a readable home, meaningful
   overlap, or explicit unresolved placement. Sources outside a narrow question
   remain available to the broader atlas.
2. **Stricter question cards:** normally 3–8 scoped question proposals per leaf,
   fewer where appropriate, with typed source contributions, actual primary
   support, methods, assumptions, regimes, conventions and limits. Description-only
   proposals are visibly provisional. A common question can gather sources that
   address it without equating complete source statements. Repeated questions
   across regions reuse one reviewed identity; neighboring but changed-premise
   questions remain distinct. Region/source-facet IDs are not canonical questions
   by fiat. Scientific implications remain separately admitted source-object edges.
3. **Independent evaluation:** retain visible development journeys, ordinary
   exploration, post-rule contrast controls and the sealed final 24-paper test as
   distinct evidence. Geometry is transductive: the unseen papers' existing
   vectors may contribute, but their descriptions and primary excerpts do not
   enter scientific review before selection freeze. Final journeys are authored
   from those sources before inspecting router output and include genuine gaps.

## Close the graph-and-embedding loop

Review outputs are construction evidence, not labels pasted on fixed clusters.
Each source unit has immutable identity and a compact assessment; a reusable factor
dictionary records system, target, regime, method or assumption, supporting unit
indices, exact description offsets/source anchors, basis, attachment scope and
uncertainty. Unknowns remain unassigned. Direct premise attachments require an
exact source-record relation; shared-paper context stays contextual.

After the coverage pass, geometry can propose reaggregation, overlap and reparenting
using these reviewed factors together with full latent affinities and admitted
source-science links. Preserve original numerical memberships and hashes as
provenance. Do not impose a universal cannot-link across different systems:
method/application/comparison routes deliberately cross them. Recompute complete
membership and coverage after moving a unit; an old review only covers its old
scope, so changed scope assignments require a short follow-up check.

Reconcile shared questions across cards through a registry of target and explicit
scope, with source-preserving aliases and redirects only after an equivalence
judgment. Typed contributions are useful even when no aliases merge. Challenge
actual assembled paths in both directions, including KK→cosmology and reverse,
alternative-route identity, method bridges, omitted rare topics and descriptions
that have no accepted canonical question. Reading coverage and remaining gaps are
part of the product, not hidden inside a completion percentage.

## Acceptance gates before scaling and before release

Every packet must resolve source IDs and hashes, exclude all24 reserved papers,
retain exact full-membership pins outside the reviewer view, and preserve source
exposure. Every reply must cover each supplied unit index exactly once; use only
its own evidence; distinguish description/excerpt basis; resolve factors and cards;
provide alternate placement for exclusions; and avoid scientific-edge promotion.
Independent challenge must specifically inspect peripheral and omitted members,
not just approve representative prose. Disagreements need an explicit disposition.

Before a full deployment, every selected leaf and parent must have a readable
reviewed card or an honest pending status, all8,473 units must remain accessible,
and only accepted scoped question identities may be shown as canonical. Review
counts, source-excerpt coverage and scientific confidence are separate measures.
Held-out tests run only after the construction and canonical registry freeze.
