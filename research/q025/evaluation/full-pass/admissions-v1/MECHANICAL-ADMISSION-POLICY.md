This policy distinguishes scientific support from final organizational admission. The KK rehearsal demonstrates the final bridge with exact coordinator decisions; it does not authorize a blanket role-to-home conversion.

1. An unchanged scope can be a supported assembly candidate when its exact proposal definition received `supported_as_written` and `scopeChange:same`, the original/derived reply and source packet hashes are pinned, and no scope-definition defect is unresolved. `supported_with_limits` plus `same` is a separate adjudication queue: retain it if the asserted limit is already explicit in the exact scope or reading limit; a newly imposed physical restriction is a new scope candidate. Format validity alone admits nothing. Source support does not establish global uniqueness or verify a reported physical result.
2. Exact independently agreed source contribution roles are admitted separately from factors, fine identities and placements. A revised factor qualification or unresolved source-statement equivalence does not erase an intact target contribution. Flagged/incomplete factors do not become premises or geometric constraints. New scope qualifications cannot be ignored just because a role string matches.
3. Stage-status copyedits must name the exact original and replacement strings and bind the resulting scope hash. Historical claims that independent review is still pending move to provenance after that review actually occurred. Actual source-reading limits remain. Workflow prose in summaries needs a bounded editorial decision; no global regex decides scientific meaning.
4. Canonical scope reconciliation chooses reusable existing destinations or proposes explicit common targets and narrower questions. It preserves all distinctions and complete group constraints. Source-statement equivalence is separate. A compound source may contribute to several questions while remaining one immutable source unit. Previously admitted destinations are fixed candidates, never omitted from the registry.
5. Organizational-home nominations may be computed from independently agreed `addresses`, `instantiates` and `specializes` roles after canonical mapping. They are NOT accepted placements. Inherited context is a counterexample: the KK linear-cosmology paper instantiates hot-brane production but belongs directly to the linear question. `method`, `comparison` and `boundary` do not nominate direct homes. Batch-r membership is provenance, not an accepted scientific home.
6. Every accepted home receives an explicit placement decision bound to the exact unit, destination scope, basis, role, weight and qualification. Existing paired target evidence can support coordinator adjudication without a new whole-source reading. A narrowly scoped organizational review is needed where target/premise compatibility is disputed, the source is compound without a clear allocation, or a fallback broad home lacks support. Equal weights organize explicitly approved multiple homes; they are not confidence, probability or duplicate source mass.
7. Final geometry uses admitted scientific destinations as bases. A broad scoped question can be a base. Redundant numerical r regions remain provenance unless explicitly retained. Contribution-only questions can have source-derived geometry anchors without receiving fabricated membership mass. Public relation direction follows EDGE-SEMANTICS.md; original native type and direction remain recorded. Review-batch links never silently become containment.

Before the semantic holdout, the exact43 reserved Qs remain unassigned in an explicitly partial8,430-Q candidate. After blind routing into frozen reviewed scopes, their placements are a dated amendment; any new scope/route is a separate repair, not an untouched holdout success.
