# Scoped question reconciliation

Canonical question count is not source-facet count. A shared scoped question may
have multiple non-equivalent source descriptions that address or instantiate it.
The atlas must reuse that destination across routes. Exact equivalence of complete
source statements is a separate and usually stricter judgment.

Candidate cases come from the union of existing MiniLM/Qwen question neighbors,
shared reviewed target/regime factors, repeated questions proposed in different
leaves, and explicit compound-target flags. Similarity nominates a case; it is
never evidence of scientific equivalence. The24 sealed papers remain excluded
from description/excerpt packets until the final freeze and test.

Each bounded case must decide one or more of these operations:

- **Common target:** author or reuse one scoped question; preserve each source's
  distinct conditions through `addresses`, `instantiates`, `specializes`, `method`,
  `boundary` or `comparison` contributions. Do not merge the source statements.
- **Scope specialization:** keep a narrower question distinct and link it to the
  broader target, recording the changed model, regime, premise or convention.
- **Destination equivalence:** merge two already authored question destinations
  only after their targets and defining conditions agree. Record redirects and
  the unchanged canonical destination independently of routes or wording.
- **Compound-source split:** preserve the immutable source description while
  assigning its different targets to separately reviewed questions. A source may
  contribute to several canonical questions; no new source facet is invented.
- **Unresolved:** preserve source access and meaningful broad homes, with an
  explicit missing premise or evidence requirement. Do not certify uniqueness.

Proposal and independent challenge inspect the same exact descriptions, relevant
primary excerpts and prior question definitions. They must include a nearby
contrast or changed-condition example and distinguish newly read primary evidence
from inherited source-review context. A revised question requires an independent
scope decision; a duplicate-looking title alone cannot authorize a redirect.

The acceptance ledger binds the exact final title, full question, scope, reading
coverage and evidence IDs with assembly's shared scope hash. Each source
contribution and destination merge/split has its own evidence and review IDs.
Nothing about a numerical bin, one source ID or a proposed singleton substitutes
for this ledger.

The initial budgeted tranche is intended to cover common targets, specializations
and compound-source cases across ordinary fields, rather than spend every case on
famous Swampland examples. Candidate and contradiction counts will be measured
from completed leaf reviews before selecting bounded batches. The existing plan's
24 reconciliation calls can be12 proposal/challenge pairs; no native launch is
made by this protocol.

Assembled checks must reach the same accepted question through two different
families, retain a genuinely narrowed destination when conditions change, and
navigate from a compound source to both targets without duplicating its source
identity. KK production, threshold microphysics and conditional cosmology remain
separate questions connected by application and premise-check routes. The sealed
final journeys are evaluated only after registry and construction freeze.
