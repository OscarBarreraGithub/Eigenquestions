# Bounded navigation decision admission policy — awaiting root authorization

This policy would admit exact agreements from a source-only proposal and a separate whole challenge. It does not admit a screen, an unanswered question, a numerical neighborhood or a complete atlas. Every admitted row remains bound to immutable packets, two distinct native review artifacts, actual reference coverage and the production scope/content hashes.

- **Scoped definitions:** admit a complete new definition only when the challenger explicitly supports it as written, the exact definition is unchanged, its source references resolve, and no unresolved defect changes its target, premises, regime or qualifications. Preserve the actual common reading basis and all source-specific reading limits. Existing definitions are reused by their admitted IDs. New wording receives a new definition identity; harmless current-status display copyediting needs an explicit content binding.
- **Typed public routes:** admit the exact endpoints, type, outgoing title, rationale and qualifications only with an explicit supported-as-written route judgment and admitted endpoint scopes. Enforce the shared direction meanings. Narrower edges must pass the containment DAG gate; method/application/premise claims remain scoped and evidenced. A comparison or related-question arc does not assert implication. Review-batch and source-object links remain separate. Reverse choice text is not invented from an outgoing title.
- **Source contributions:** admit an exact proposed unit–target–role only with its own explicit independent row judgment. Both source descriptions and actual common own-paper primary references determine the reading basis; inherited notes are context, not fresh excerpts. Preserve both readers' qualifications. No home, mass, source-statement identity or USING relation follows. A compound source may contribute to one broad destination or several narrower ones without resolving atomic identities.
- **Authored-question equality:** admit a complete explicitly reviewed group only when one exact admitted target scope represents every member, every internal pair has current same-scope support, and the authoritative global inventory has no unresolved distinction, specialization or changed-premise constraint. Run the whole-group production gate; never union positive pairs transitively. Preserve all original scope/source qualifications and both reading limits. Equality of authored questions does not assert equality of their source statements. Home reassignment requires the separately approved scope-compatible placement mapping.
- **Other operations:** supported common-target reuse, specialization, distinct-child organization and negative distinctions retain their exact meanings. They do not silently become aliases or public routes. Public routes require their separate explicit judgments. Different identity does not mean no useful scientific connection.

Explicitly supported prior decisions and routes form the effective graph without being re-emitted. Changed decisions require new IDs and a recorded supersession; unknown or insufficiently supported references cannot become effective. Exact repeated serialization creates no second logical object. Every prior question/decision, proposed route and proposed new source-role row requires its own challenge assessment.

Exceptions remain pending: changed/rejected definitions or routes; challenger-only additions; missing coverage; incomplete scientific qualifications; source/premise conflicts; unsupported primary attribution; unresolved global identity conflicts; and the six retained screen-variant conflicts. Small consequential exceptions use the existing exact descriptions/notes first and bounded primary passages only where needed. No blanket whole-paper reread is required.

The coordinator reviews representative complete journeys and these exception classes, rather than every unchanged row. Final release still requires complete source-home coverage, resolved current identity mappings, readable multi-step navigation, frozen candidate testing and the separately disclosed semantic holdout procedure.
