The exact current direction meanings are:

- `narrower`: source is the broader scoped question; target is a genuine refinement. Sharing a method or selected component is insufficient for full-scope containment.
- `method_for`: source supplies a method or methodological question applicable to the target question under the stated conditions. A target that instead supplies the method requires an explicit reversal. Keep limited background, spectrum, dimensional, perturbative and boundary assumptions.
- `application_of`: source is the applied scientific question; target is the underlying method, mechanism or construction being applied. The old word `application` did not fix this direction. Do not turn a broad topic association into an application claim.
- `premise_check`: source is the conditional inference, construction or control question; target asks about a prerequisite, applicability or reliability condition relevant to it. A broad source does not acquire every target premise universally. A premise result affecting a downstream application does not establish this forward arrow from premise to application.
- `comparison`: source and target are explicitly compared with their differences and non-transfer limits. It does not assert implication, equivalence or incompatibility.
- `related_question`: an explicitly supported scientific connection whose existing reasoning does not justify one of the stronger types. Preserve the actual useful connection; do not routinely flatten clear methods or applications into this type.
