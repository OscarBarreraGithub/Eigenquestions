# Exact uncomplicated organizational homes

Root authorized this class after reading ten full source/scope examples and the KK counterexamples on 2026-11-17. This is a coordinator policy decision based on existing independently agreed evidence; the original reviewers did not separately adjudicate the derived organizational placement.

Admit weight one to an unchanged, already admitted scope only when both reviewers assign `addresses`, both source dispositions are `member`, and the unit has exactly one positive target in each review and one agreed `addresses` destination across the pinned admission cut. Exclude unresolved identity, scope/regime conflict, unresolved annotation flags, inherited/context-only or unclear/compound targets. Retain the actual common reading basis and exact source/scope provenance.

Membership can be in a broader question. It does not assert equality between the source statement and the destination, full-paper verification, canonical uniqueness, or scientific implication. `instantiates`, `specializes`, multiple positive targets, changed premises and ambiguous homes remain outside this authorization. Other independent comparison/method annotations are not positive targets, although their actual qualifications still matter.

Canonical remapping requires separately admitted identity decisions. A changed canonical scope must revalidate the placement. This fragment supplies exact homes for its listed units only; it neither admits a complete atlas nor creates public branches, aliases or routes. The root-approved examples were sampling aids, not an ontology.
