## Round-three counterexamples to a size-only choice

The preserve-candidates variant improves all ordinary-probe reading burdens
(compactification median 69; inflation 39). The extra-facet-context variant gives
104 and 58, with better scattering/holography burdens (57 and 47). These remain
coverage/readability proxies, not success scores.

A particularly revealing preserved fine region,
`multiview_preserve_candidates:region:0403944f92af04eaed4a`, has only nine units and
contains 2311.05318 and 2610.01825, but its second representative is
`astro-ph/0703099`, the historical HiRes cosmic-ray spectral suppression/GZK
question. It fails as an exact KK-graviton region despite excellent witness recall
and apparent specificity. Keep this false-positive boundary case in the next
review; do not simply relabel the nine units from the KK witnesses.

Likewise `multiview_preserve_candidates:region:431e39adaf172dcc211a` (21 units)
connects rapid-turn inflation to DBI/sound-speed/perturbation methods and includes
0803.0859, whose source question explicitly asks for density fluctuations **without
inflation**. A method or broader cosmological-perturbation relation can make sense;
an unconditional inflation child does not.

Extra-facet context makes a different useful tradeoff: 1605.08052's gauge-realization
question appears in a 146-unit F-theory charge/spectrum/geometric-realizability
region (`8d9d4d04cfaed30bba4e`), whereas the earlier multiview region emphasized
anomalies. That may better match this source's target. Conversely, its KK
cosmology sources fall in a 76-unit relic-abundance/thermal-history region
(`1a59e9c1af68ec589759`) whose representatives are mostly thermal freeze-out,
WIMPless hidden sectors and axion inflation. They remain connected, but the target
is less immediately legible. Retain candidate overlaps and assess scientific
scope case by case instead of declaring a universal numerical winner.
