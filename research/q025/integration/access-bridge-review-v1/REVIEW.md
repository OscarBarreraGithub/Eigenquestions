# Independent review of proposed source-supported access

**The narrow organizational bridge is justified as a navigation aid. It is not
enough by itself to establish the complete public graph.** An allocated source
question belongs to a reviewed broad home and has an independently admitted
positive contribution to another scope. That exact incidence is useful for
finding a related question, including a supported question with no primary mass.
It adds no allocation, scope containment, identity, applicability or physical
implication. The two underlying admission decisions should remain inspectable.

I verified all five diagnostic input hashes and all **3,774** support joins across
**1,042** candidates. Every witness resolves to the stated accepted, positive
allocation and exact contribution decision; qualifications agree. There are no
duplicate endpoint pairs or duplicated units within a route. The witnesses are
2,066 distinct source units; 409 routes have only one witness. This is an audit of
the join, not another primary-source review or navigation admission.

## Concrete boundaries

1. **One candidate violates the question-only class.** `region:88fa8687…` (de
   Sitter existence/instability) reaches `region:953776ce…` (vacuum-stability
   statistics), supported by `unit:a83c7372…` and
   `decision:canonical-explicit-role:statistics:z57`. The qualification explicitly
   separates conditional IIA trial-search probability from the IIB ensemble.
   Either root explicitly admits scope-to-scope access or this candidate must be
   excluded from a question-only rule. It must not appear as an accepted common
   ensemble or universal stability probability.
2. **Compound source support does not transfer all premises.** The broad Dark
   Dimension/cosmic-ray home reaches the UHECR-cutoff/gravitational-scale question
   through `unit:72ea9aab…`. Its two retained readings explicitly distinguish
   cutoff compatibility/universality from a *conditional* species-scale inference.
   An organizational link is useful; “Dark Dimension explains the cutoff” or a
   containment arrow would overstate it. Likewise, a contribution to cosmological
   fits (`unit:c08ed44e…`) expressly depends on calibration and BAO choices, not
   universal simultaneous tension resolution.
3. **A positive role is not a proof.** `unit:68b2460d…` supplies a black-hole
   setting and entropy-test apparatus to an extremality/consistency question.
   Its qualification says this is not an independent proof and keeps inherited
   graph qualifications local to object `o96`. The bridge must retain that limit,
   not promote it into a whole-region physical assertion. Existing abbreviated
   review qualifications must remain honestly attributed; do not fabricate a
   missing ending or call them complete primary conditions.
4. **Candidate reachability is not a containment or release metric.** The current
   probe improves Dark-sectors access from 63/123 allocated source questions to
   118/123, and black-hole access from 26/73 to 70/73. It uses not-yet-admitted
   paired native links and permits incoming traversal. The five Dark-sector
   residuals include the separately admitted historical HiRes question. An old
   numerical child inventory is not a reason to force a scientific Dark-sector
   link to HiRes. Global discoverability needs its appropriate entry, not a false
   ancestor. Recompute reachability on the actual admitted graph after residual
   placements and reserved-source treatment; this 5,765-source diagnostic cannot
   certify the complete release.

## Minimal admission and display conditions

- Use **direct accepted broad-home allocation**, with positive weight, and exact
  `unitId` equality. Do not substitute a same-paper method, inherited ancestor
  membership, support centroid, numerical neighbor or an earlier proposed home.
  Only the admitted `addresses`/`instantiates`/`specializes` row participates.
  Pin both endpoint scope versions, allocation/contribution decisions, evidence
  basis and qualification. Revalidate the join after scope or identity amendments.
- `H_broadᵀ R_positive` is an incidence construction here. A nonzero entry means
  an explicitly witnessed access path; it is not a confidence, probability,
  physical coupling or new H mass. Count unique supporting units, keeping their
  roles separate. Do not propagate these links transitively into new assertions,
  ancestor contributions or containment/overview mass.
- Mark the route as **organizational access through a shared source**, even if
  transported using existing `related_question` plus explicit provenance. Keep
  it distinguishable from independently reviewed scientific premise/application
  routes and subordinate to those when menus are crowded. Retain all links in
  paging. Incoming browsing is allowed with an incoming-access label, not the
  converse of the source role or scientific direction.
- The route witness count is separate from destination membership. In a USING,
  method-lens or year-filtered view, an ON question witness may not match the
  selected sources. Either keep explicitly scope-wide navigation with its global
  witness count, or compute a genuinely complete filtered witness count. Never
  relabel the bridge as USING applicability, silently filter only representatives,
  or hide a zero-primary-mass destination that still has admitted contributions.

These conditions preserve meaningful exploration without inventing a hierarchy.
No bridge implementation or admission was made in this review. The exact IDs and
qualifications for the examples are retained in `audit.json`.
