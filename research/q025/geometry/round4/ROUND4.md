The 186-leaf `multiview_preserve_candidates` construction is selected as the **proposal basis for full source review**, not an accepted final clustering. Its bounded leaves and explicit low-support branches make review practical. Extra-facet, global-Qwen and local-consensus candidates remain alternative boundary nominations. The competing construction is a typed factor graph, rather than another attraction-weight grid. Exact source-description spans nominate physical systems, frameworks, targets and regimes. Shared factors offer different kinds of routes; reviewed source-method relations remain directed, scoped records in a separate layer. Raw geometric neighbors remain available. No shared factor merges canonical questions or establishes an implication. Forty-five nominated factors produce 303 question-to-factor annotations.

For a bounded ordering experiment, specific shared systems, specific frameworks, specific targets and broad targets form successive priority tiers; cosine orders peers within a tier. This deliberately exposes the cost of a simple role priority. It is a development hypothesis, not a universal ranking policy or a scientifically certified ontology.

The results favor **reviewed semantic factors plus preserved latent alternatives**, not shipping regex scopes as scientific truth. No GPU re-encoding is justified by this round alone.

## Scoped method controls and limits

All three admitted development method controls remain available with exact source-object endpoints, original assertion direction, applicability and conventions: D4 flux interpolation across AdS4/AdS3 systems, Einstein–DeTurck gauge fixing across matter systems, and projected forward dispersion for one-/multi-photon states. Browsing an incoming edge does not assert the converse. These controls are prior source reviews, not held-out discoveries.

Rules and cohort were frozen before reading the three public post-rule control prompts. Predictions in `results/post-rule-predictions.json` use generic exact-endpoint lookup in the current reviewed ledger; positive and nonpositive outcomes are separated. Reserved expected labels and the sealed final paper holdout were not read. This is a type/scope/receipt audit using existing knowledge, not blind-source generalization. Independent grading belongs to the evaluation agent.

The independent challenge in `evaluation/factor-review/challenge-v1.json` passed all three receipt-preservation controls, with exactly that limited interpretation. It identified eight description-span problems, including a false affirmative inflation factor for `1007.0027` asking whether a Galileon phase replaces inflation; lost phase/negation qualifiers in `2205.06065`; missing entropy-kinetic and radiation-preinflation/initial-state targets in `1807.05240` and `2005.12370`; missing sound-speed/spectrum comparison routes for `0803.0859` and `0811.3633`; and the varying-curvature question in `1901.08603`, which challenges a restriction rather than universally assuming it. The frozen rules and outputs are retained unchanged. These errors reject promotion of the lexical factor draft, while motivating evidence-qualified factors from the full scientific review.

Full-corpus method audit found that **51 of 97 admitted reviewed source relations touch an exact browser-method node, and 50 have both endpoints represented**. The question-only corpus had zero endpoint matches. The additive source-context index will make these real method routes available without projecting relations through paper identity or inferring question equivalence.
