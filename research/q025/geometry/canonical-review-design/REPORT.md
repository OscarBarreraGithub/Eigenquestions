# Canonical review workload design

**Use a compact nomination-triage pass, then source-rich paired review for consequential cases. Do not launch the uniform 576-call draft.** The 20-scope/40-pair bounds are chosen batching heuristics, not measured model limits. The 280,000-character input bound reserves room for long proposal/challenge outputs. Most current packets reach that input ceiling while averaging only 12.3 scopes and 14.5 comparisons.

The held rich draft repeats 1,112 scopes across 3,538 appearances, 5,743 descriptions across 36,508 appearances, and 2,209 primary windows across 11,938 appearances. It contains 76.4 million input characters. Ten final packets slightly exceed the nominal input ceiling; the maximum is 280,949. The 576-call draft remains unlaunched. Root separately launched four compact triage pilots after these measurements; that is not a launch of the held rich draft.

Every tested alternative retains all 1,112 scopes and 4,168 queued workload IDs: 3,772 nomination IDs plus 396 weak contrasts for 132 unmatched scopes. Fifteen duplicate unordered comparisons share one comparison record but retain every original workload ID. Eighteen active-pilot nominations remain explicitly deferred, accounting for the full cumulative 3,790 nominations. A packet is a reading workload, not a scientific community or common parent.

Direct repacking alone saves little under the rich-evidence policy. Most savings come from avoiding repeated primary text and moving long IDs, hashes and locator maps into exact machine sidecars. Scientific scope strings and whole descriptions are not shortened. The compact views retain the full prior **scope-assessment** note and reading limit, source-role agreements and material-scope flags. They do **not** contain both reviewers' per-unit attribution notes; those are available in the pinned original packets and the separate home audit. Therefore these views are prioritization inputs, not complete paired source-context reviews. Missing inherited/compound/premise qualifications must escalate rather than be guessed. The four launched pilot inputs are unchanged; an additive per-unit-note measurement can be evaluated separately.

The 56-packet export is `triage-export-index.json`; its ignored `.local.json` views total 15.02MB and sidecars 29.05MB. Maximum view size is 279,588 characters. Export checks exact definition/method/assessment-note equality and every source-description SHA after serialization. Repeated scope appearances are comparison context; each scope has one accounting anchor. Two different `PYTHONHASHSEED` runs produce identical `packings.json`, SHA `80c6abe4e8304eadd5df34133ccd052dce8eeb617861e47ffb195bffee59bc17`.

Use the triage output to distinguish identity screening from useful connection screening. “Distinct targets” does not imply “no method, application, comparison or broader-target route.” Keep an explicit outcome or pending status for every workload ID and every unmatched scope. A single triage pass is 56 calls; two passes would be 112, but neither count predicts the later rich-review workload. Do not substitute a presumed escalation percentage for actual pilot outcomes or equate an unreviewed negative with unique canonical identity.

Escalate plausible reuse/equivalence, new canonical scopes, changed-premise conflicts and consequential new routes into small paired packets. Supply the relevant original descriptions, complete inherited source notes, the specific primary windows needed, existing admitted scopes/routes, and all known pair constraints touching a proposed group. Reuse already-supported unchanged definitions and existing receipt bindings. Global admission still checks whole proposed canonical groups against distinction/specialization constraints; common-target grouping never becomes an equivalence union. Do not reread every paper merely because one source participates in many comparisons.

The four nominated pilots cover dense Gauss–Bonnet inflation/late-time targets (`0006`), mediation/regime neighbors (`0019`), distinct black-hole targets (`0022`), and admitted KK/UHECR weak or unmatched contrasts (`0028`). These are nomination rationales, not answer keys. Their output and cognitive capacity remain an empirical pilot gate: 60/200 is a packing result, not a demonstrated semantic limit.

`measure_packings.py` reproduces eight executed alternatives from the exact held input index and already-prepared packets. `export_triage.py` writes the checked views/sidecars without launching a model. No archive pages or sealed holdout files were opened for this design experiment; prepared primary text was counted and deduplicated as data, not claimed as newly read scientific evidence. The frozen 25-file real-rehearsal checkpoint was not modified.
