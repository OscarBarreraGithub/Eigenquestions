Research taste quiz

This quiz runs a topic tournament. First it compares all broad fields until every top-level field has appeared enough times. Then it drills only into the fields and branches you ranked highest, pruning the rest. Finally it makes cross-field comparisons among the surviving high-rated areas.

About thirty broad-field comparisons, followed by focused drill-down and cross-field topic comparisons. For each pair, pick whichever research direction you find more interesting — not more important, just the one you'd rather think about.

Honest disclaimer: this is an exploratory profile, not psychometrics — keyword-derived noisy proxy axes over a hep-ph corpus, no reliability or validity claims. It's a conversation piece.

    Caveats (ship these with the result)

    • This is an exploratory profile, not psychometrics — no test–retest reliability, no construct validity.
    • Every axis is built on keyword-derived noisy proxy labels, not clean semantic truth; the exact axis directions are not unique.
    • D/T (Data ↔ Theory) overlaps a confounded anchor — it may track subfield more than temperament. Least reliable.
    • H/Z (Phenomenon ↔ Formalism) is single-family-dominant; treat it as the next least reliable.
    • The adaptive policy concentrates on your high-interest branches, so the axis estimate is not an unbiased sweep — that's the point, but it biases the estimate toward the areas you kept choosing.
    • This measures self-reported taste, not revealed behavior.
