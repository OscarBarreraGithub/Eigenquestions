    About this fixed map

    This overview uses a frozen text-affinity layout of source question excerpts from a Swampland-centered research collection. Panning, zooming and Find change only the display. They do not change the groups or update the separate text explorer.

    Topic names and memberships are provisional, agent-proposed organization aids. The stored hierarchy is shallow: only the groups with actual children show finer groups. Unassigned excerpts remain available separately. Group paper counts overlap and should not be added.

    Positions are a standardized projection of text-affinity geometry, not physical distances. The chosen question view has one connected component. Any displayed topic links express coarse text affinity, not citations or physical implications.
